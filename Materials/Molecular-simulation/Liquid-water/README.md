# Centroid Molecular Dynamics of Liquid Water

## 🧠 Overview
This project explores the application of rigid-body Centroid Molecular Dynamics (CMD) to liquid water, specifically utilizing the TIP4P model. By employing a more efficient computational approach than standard flexible CMD, this research characterizes how quantization influences the structural and dynamical properties of water across a wide range of temperatures, including the supercooled regime.

## 🔬 Core Analyses
* **Isotopic & Quantum Effects:** Comparative study of equilibrium and dynamical properties in liquid $H_2O$ and $D_2O$.
* **Rotational & Librational Motion:** Impact of quantization on the angular degrees of freedom and the "effective tunneling" of water molecules.
* **Thermal Uncertainty:** Investigation of the unique, non-monotonic behavior of quantum mechanical uncertainty as a function of temperature.

## 🛠 Methodology
The simulations utilize a **rigid-body CMD technique**, which provides significant computational efficiency over standard flexible models while maintaining high accuracy. The approach involves both classical and quantum simulations to isolate the effects of quantization, specifically looking at how the strong water-water interactions in the condensed phase influence the quantum mechanical uncertainty of the molecules.
