Quantum / Molecular Theory

Overview

This research area focuses on theoretical and computational approaches to quantum dynamics, statistical mechanics, condensed-phase quantum systems, and molecular-level response theory. The work explores how quantum mechanical behavior emerges in complex many-body systems and how quantum effects evolve toward classical behavior under non-equilibrium and condensed-phase conditions.

Several projects investigate quantum dynamics in condensed matter, non-equilibrium thermodynamics, quantum free-energy methods, and correlation-function formalisms. Additional studies examine spectroscopy and lattice dynamics in solid-state materials, connecting molecular-scale theory with experimentally observable properties.

This section also includes exploratory work in quantum computing and quantum-information concepts, reflecting broader interests in modern computational and theoretical frameworks for quantum systems.
