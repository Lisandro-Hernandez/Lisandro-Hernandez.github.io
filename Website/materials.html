Materials Modeling & Molecular Simulation

Overview

This research area focuses on theoretical and computational modeling of materials, molecular systems, and condensed matter phenomena using methods from statistical mechanics, thermodynamics, and quantum theory.

The projects investigate equilibrium and non-equilibrium behavior in molecular and materials systems, including phase equilibrium, aqueous ionic chemistry, quantum free energy methods, and spectroscopic properties of solids and complex materials.

Several studies combine atomistic modeling with thermodynamic response theory to analyze transport phenomena, quantum correlation functions, and the microscopic origins of macroscopic material behavior. The work spans both classical and quantum descriptions of matter across multiple length and time scales.
