Electrochemistry & Energy Systems

My work in electrochemistry focuses on the physics of ion transport, interfacial kinetics, and energy conversion processes, with particular emphasis on lithium-based systems and nonlinear electrochemical dynamics.

The central goal is to connect continuum transport theory with interfacial reaction mechanisms, enabling predictive models of electrochemical behavior under equilibrium and far-from-equilibrium conditions.

These studies range from classical conductivity theory in electrolytes to dynamic moving-boundary problems in energy systems.
