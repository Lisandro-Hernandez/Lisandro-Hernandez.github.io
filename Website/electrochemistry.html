Electrochemistry / Energy Systems

Overview

Research focused on ion transport, electrochemical kinetics, conductivity phenomena, and energy conversion systems using continuum and molecular-level models.

Topics include equilibrium conductivity, nonlinear transport, moving boundary methods, Butler–Volmer kinetics, and thermodynamic descriptions of electrochemical systems.

Several projects overlap with kinetic transport, materials modeling, and molecular simulation, reflecting the interconnected nature of the research ecosystem.
