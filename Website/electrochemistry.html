Electrochemistry / Energy Systems

Overview

This research area focuses on theoretical and computational modeling of electrochemical systems, ion transport, conductivity phenomena, and energy conversion processes. The work combines equilibrium thermodynamics, kinetic transport theory, and nonlinear interfacial dynamics to analyze electrochemical behavior across multiple scales.

Several projects investigate transport processes in ionic systems, including diffusion, conductivity, moving boundary methods, and nonlinear electrochemical kinetics. Other studies examine thermodynamic aspects of electrochemical systems and energy transport.

The projects were primarily developed as part of advanced instructional and independent modeling work, with emphasis on physics-based simulation, continuum modeling, and numerical analysis.
