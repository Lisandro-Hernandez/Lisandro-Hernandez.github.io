Materials Modeling & Molecular Simulation

Atomistic and statistical mechanical modeling of materials, phase behavior, and condensed matter systems.

Phase Equilibrium Modeling in Multi-Component Systems

Simulated phase behavior of single-component and binary systems using statistical mechanics and molecular modeling to predict thermodynamic equilibrium properties.

Solid-State Spectroscopy and Lattice Dynamics

Modeled lattice dynamics in solid-state systems to predict spectroscopic observables, including Mössbauer spectra, linking atomic-scale motion to measurable material response.

Reaction Kinetics in Continuous Stirred-Tank Reactors (CSTR Modeling)

Modeled transient reaction kinetics in continuous stirred-tank reactors (CSTR) to study time-dependent behavior, stability, and dynamic response in chemical process systems.

Quantum & Computational Methods

Quantum Dynamics of Condensed-Phase Systems

Developed simulation approaches for quantum dynamics in condensed-phase systems, focusing on the emergence of classical behavior using semi-classical methods.

Non-equilibrium Free Energy Calculations

Computed quantum free energy differences using non-equilibrium path integral methods to study thermodynamic behavior beyond equilibrium conditions.

Quantum Correlation Functions and Material Response

Investigated quantum time correlation functions to analyze material response and thermodynamic constraints in complex systems.
