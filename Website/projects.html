Projects

Quantum Dynamics & Theoretical Work

Quantum mechanical modeling of dynamical processes, thermodynamics, and non-equilibrium phenomena.

Quantum Dynamics of Condensed-Phase Systems (Quantum-to-Classical Transition)

Developed simulation approaches to study quantum dynamics in condensed-phase systems, focusing on the emergence of classical behavior from quantum mechanical descriptions using path integral and semi-classical methods.

Non-equilibrium Free Energy Calculations

Computed quantum free energy differences using non-equilibrium path integral methods to investigate thermodynamic properties beyond equilibrium conditions.

Quantum Time Correlation Functions and Material Response

Analyzed quantum correlation functions to understand material response properties under thermodynamic constraints and external perturbations.

Materials Modeling & Molecular Simulation

Atomistic and statistical mechanical modeling of materials, phase behavior, and molecular systems.

Solid-State Spectroscopy and Lattice Dynamics Modeling

Modeled lattice dynamics in solid-state systems to predict spectroscopic properties, including Mössbauer spectra, linking microscopic structure to measurable observables.

Phase Equilibrium Modeling in Multi-Component Systems

Simulated phase behavior of single-component and binary mixtures using molecular simulation and thermodynamic models to predict equilibrium properties.

Aqueous Multi-Equilibrium Acid–Base Systems

Developed models for complex aqueous acid–base equilibria, enabling prediction of titration curves and multi-species solution behavior.

Kinetic Processes & Computational Engineering

Modeling of reaction dynamics, transport processes, and biological/chemical systems.

Transient Reaction Dynamics in Chemical Reactors (CSTR)

Modeled transient reaction kinetics in continuous stirred-tank reactors to analyze time-dependent chemical process behavior and stability.

Multi-Pathway Drug Transport and Pharmacokinetics Modeling

Developed kinetic models of drug transport, degradation, and accumulation across competing biological pathways.

Anticancer Drug Pharmacokinetics (Erlotinib Distribution and Degradation)

Modeled pharmacokinetics of Erlotinib to study distribution, degradation, and system-level drug behavior in biological environments.
