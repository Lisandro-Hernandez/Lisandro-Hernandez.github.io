Kinetic & Transport Processes

Overview

This research area focuses on kinetic phenomena, transport theory, and time-dependent processes in chemical, biological, and condensed-phase systems. The work combines theoretical modeling, numerical simulation, and computational methods to analyze transient and non-equilibrium behavior across multiple physical scales.

Several projects investigate chemical reaction dynamics, transport processes, and competing kinetic pathways, including transient reactor behavior, pharmacokinetic transport, degradation mechanisms, and accumulation phenomena in complex systems.

Additional work explores quantum dynamics, non-equilibrium thermodynamics, and response theory in condensed-phase systems, connecting microscopic dynamical descriptions with macroscopic transport and relaxation behavior. These projects overlap naturally with molecular simulation, electrochemistry, and quantum theoretical modeling.
